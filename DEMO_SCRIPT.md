# Kudzu demo script

A five-minute walkthrough that shows every feature in order.

**Before you start**

1. Run `npm run dev` and open the app on a laptop, so it shows inside the phone frame.
2. Open the demo controls (wrench button, or tap the Kudzu logo three times) and choose **Reset all demo data**.
3. You should be on the welcome screen.

The demo controls are referred to below as **Demo**.

---

## 0:00 Onboarding (40 seconds)

"Kudzu matches students and alumni of the same school for coffee chats."

1. Tap **Verify with your .edu email**.
2. Type `riley@gatech.edu`. Point out that Georgia Tech is detected and selected from the email domain. Mention the other schools in the list, the alum verification path, and **My school isn't here yet**.
3. Tap **Continue** once to show the Student / Alum step. Mention that sign-up also asks who you **already know**, so nobody starts with an empty network.
4. Say that the remaining steps collect basics, **what you're looking for and what you can offer**, three prompts and preferences. Nobody picks "mentor" or "mentee": the app works that out. Then tap **Skip** (top right) to load the prefilled demo user, Riley, a third-year Industrial Engineering student.

## 0:40 Meet (70 seconds)

"This is the core of the app. It works like Hinge, but for networking."

1. Scroll Maya's card. Start with the green **Learn from** box: it says why she is being shown ("Maya can help with…"), and the topics she offers that Riley asked for are highlighted. Then **spots left this month**, the **In common** strip and her three prompts.
2. Tap the **heart** on "Ask me about…". Type a comment such as *I'd love to hear more about switching from finance to UX!* and tap **Send like with comment**.
3. The **match screen** appears. (Maya always matches. Other right swipes match about 40% of the time.)
4. Tap **Keep browsing**.
5. Drag the next card left to pass, and tap the coffee button to connect with the one after.
6. Tap the **filter icon**: grad-year range, industries, distance, "Traveling to…", Student / Alum / Both. Under **Kind of match**, tap **Give back** and show the results: the deck is now younger students Riley can help, each with a **Give back** box. Clear the filter.
7. Tap the **two-person icon** to turn on **Bring a friend**. Show the double coffee chat card, tap **Request group chat**, then turn the mode off.

## 1:50 Messages (60 seconds)

1. Open the **Messages** tab. Point out the **New matches** row and the conversation list.
2. Open **Maya**. Your prompt comment is the first message, and she has replied.
3. Tap the **coffee icon** next to the message box. Pick two or three times and a place, then **Send proposal**. After a moment Maya accepts and the card becomes **Coffee chat confirmed**.
4. Go back and open **Marcus Lee**. He proposed times to you. Tap **Accept** on one.
5. Tap the **⋯** menu in the header to show **Report / Block**. Close it.

## 2:50 Network (70 seconds)

"This is the signature feature. Your network is a living thing."

1. Open the **Network** tab. Read out one or two **nudges**: Jordan is in town this week, Sam just started at Google.
2. Tap **Message** on the Sam nudge. The chat opens with a congratulation already drafted. Go back.
3. Scroll the list. Connections are grouped by strength with **leaf icons**: Strong, Steady, Fading, Dormant. Each also carries a tag for the kind of relationship: **Learning from**, **Trading notes** or **Giving back**. Tap **Giving back** in the chip row to show Diego, a first-year Riley has helped. At the top, open **Class of 2028** to show where the class has ended up and the class event, then tap **I know them** on a classmate and say when you last talked. They join the network with the right leaf.
4. Tap **Map**. Vines grow out from your root marker to each city. Stronger connections get thicker, greener vines. Fading ones are thin and dashed.
5. Tap the **Seattle cluster**. The other vines dim and a card lists the people there, with message and coffee buttons. Tap **Zoom in** to see individual leaf pins.
6. Switch back to **List** and open **Jordan Alvarez**: strength, interaction timeline and a private note.
7. **Demo → Fast-forward one month.** Back on Network, the counts at the top shift toward Fading, and new nudges appear.

## 4:00 Events and groups (40 seconds)

1. Open the **Events** tab. Show **For You** against **All**, and tap a filter chip such as **Virtual**. Clear it.
2. Open **Midtown Morning Coffee Meetup**. Show the map pin, the RSVP buttons, and the **guest list**. Tap a guest you haven't met, such as **Sofia Marin**, to open their full profile with a **Connect** button. Go back twice.
3. Tap **Host** to show the form: title, type, date, location, capacity, public or community-only. Close it.
4. Tap the **Groups** tab. Join a group, then open one to see its members and events.

## 4:40 Feedback, thank-yous and profile (40 seconds)

1. **Demo → Trigger a post-chat check-in.** Answer *Did you meet?*, give a thumbs up and pick a tag. Stress that these answers are **private and never shown on a profile**.
2. Scroll to **Send a thank-you**, type one line and submit. This is the one part the other person sees. Open their chat to show the thank-you card and their reply. "Research shows a thank-you is what brings a mentor back."
3. **Demo → Receive a thank-you note.** The Profile tab opens on **Thank-you notes**, which only Riley can see.
4. **Demo → Show account-warning notice.** Show the gentle notice on Profile and tap it to open the community guidelines.
5. On Profile, point out edit profile, chat capacity, groups, events and **Pause my profile**.

## Closing: any school (10 seconds)

1. **Demo → Switch school → Emory.** Flip through Meet, Events and Network. Everything is now Emory.
2. **Demo → Switch user** to show the alum view.

"Kudzu works for any school. Schools are data, not code."

---

## If something goes wrong

- **Stuck or cluttered state:** Demo → **Reset all demo data**, then **Skip onboarding (demo)**.
- **Meet deck is empty:** tap **See everyone again**, or widen the filters.
- **Map is grey:** map tiles need an internet connection. The pins and vines still draw without them.
- **No confirmed chat for the check-in:** propose a coffee chat in any conversation first. It is accepted automatically.
