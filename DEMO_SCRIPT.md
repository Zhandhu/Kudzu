# Kudzu demo script

A five-minute walkthrough that shows every feature in order.

**Before you start**

1. Run `npm run dev` and open the app on a laptop, so it shows inside the phone frame.
2. Open the demo controls (wrench button, or tap the Kudzu logo three times) and choose **Reset all demo data**.
3. You should be on the welcome screen.

The demo controls are referred to below as **Demo**.

---

## 0:00 Onboarding (40 seconds)

"Kudzu connects students and alumni of the same school, and helps them stay connected."

1. Tap **Verify with your .edu email**.
2. Type `riley@gatech.edu`. Point out that Georgia Tech is detected and selected from the email domain. Mention the other schools in the list, the alum verification path, and **My school isn't here yet**.
3. Tap **Continue** once to show the Student / Alum step. Mention that sign-up also asks who you **already know**, so nobody starts with an empty network.
4. Say that the remaining steps collect basics, **what you're looking for and what you can offer**, three prompts, and **who you want to find**: fields, cities and career level. Those answers decide who is shown first and never hide anyone. Nobody picks "mentor" or "mentee": the app works that out. Then tap **Skip** (top right) to load the prefilled demo user, Riley, a third-year Industrial Engineering student.

## 0:40 Find (70 seconds)

"This is where you find people. You say what you need, and the school's network answers."

1. The tab opens on **What are you looking for?** with Riley's own asks ready to tap. Under it are a few people who can help, each drawn as a **leaf**. The line beneath shows who Riley already knows that knows them.
2. Tap **Maya O.** once. A summary appears: her role, why she is shown, people in common, how many spots she has left and how quickly she replies. Tap **Open full profile**.
3. On her profile, point to the green **Learn from** box and her three prompts. Tap **Ask Maya for a chat**. Pick what it is about, type one line and tap **Send request**. "No swiping, and no waiting for a mutual match. You ask, with a reason."
4. After a moment the bar changes to **Message** and **Propose coffee**: Maya has accepted. (Maya always accepts. Others accept about 60% of the time, and some never answer, so that case can be shown.)
5. Go back to Find. Type **consult** in the search bar and tap the **Consulting** chip it offers. Results **grow on branches**, grouped by city. Tap **Level** to regroup them. Leaves are coloured by relationship: learn from, trade notes, give back. Outlined leaves are people already in Riley's network.
6. Tap the **+** in the search bar to show the other ways to narrow: field, city, level and **Can help with**. Close it and tap **Clear all**.
7. Tap **Simple** (top right) to show the same results as a plain list. Tap **Leaves** to go back.
8. Point to the two leaves in the bottom-right corner. Tap the **tendril**, which is **Requests**:
   - **Asked of you** are requests from others. Each can be answered in one tap: **Accept**, **Later on** or **Not right now**.
   - **Waiting on** are Riley's own. One reads "Rachel couldn't get to this one," with a button to **see who else can help**. "A request never just goes quiet."
9. Tap the **brown leaf**, **Not yet met**: Nina and Kwame have accepted but Riley has not met them yet, so they are not in the network. Tapping a name opens the chat. The cup proposes coffee.

## 1:50 Messages (60 seconds)

1. Open the **Messages** tab and look down the conversation list.
2. Open **Maya**. The line from your request is the first message, and she has replied.
3. Tap the **coffee icon** next to the message box. Pick two or three times and a place, then **Send proposal**. After a moment Maya accepts and the card becomes **Coffee chat confirmed**.
4. Go back and open **Marcus Lee**. He proposed times to you. Tap **Accept** on one.
5. Tap the **⋯** menu in the header to show **Report / Block**. Close it.

## 2:50 Network (70 seconds)

"This is the signature feature. Your network is a living thing."

1. Open the **Network** tab. It opens on the **map**. A vine grows from your root marker to each city. Each vine is a tangle of strands, one per person there, coloured by how close you are: deep green for strong, pale and dashed for fading.
2. Tap the **Seattle cluster**. The other vines dim and a card lists the people there, with message and coffee buttons. Tap **Zoom in**: the vine arrives at the city and splits into a branch for each person's leaf.
3. Point to the **two leaves** in the bottom-right corner. The purple blossom shows how many things are waiting, and it darkens as the number rises.
4. Tap the first leaf, **Reach out**. Read out one or two nudges: Jordan is in town this week, Sam just started at Google. Tap **Message** on the Sam nudge. The chat opens with a congratulation already drafted. Go back.
5. Tap the second leaf, **Grow**. Open **Class of 2028** to show where the class has ended up and the class event, then tap **I know them** on a classmate and say when you last talked. They join the network with the right leaf. Go back.
6. Switch to **List**. Connections are grouped by strength with leaf icons, and each carries a tag for the kind of relationship: **Learning from**, **Trading notes** or **Giving back**. Tap **Giving back** in the chip row to show Diego, a first-year Riley has helped.
7. Open **Devon Brooks**. Tap **What this is based on**: closeness adds up every interaction, a good coffee chat counts most, and each one fades with time. Below it, **Keeping in touch** shows the monthly rhythm Riley chose.
8. **Demo → Fast-forward one month.** Back on Network, the counts at the top shift toward Fading. Open **Reach out**: the first nudge is the monthly catch-up with Devon.

## 4:00 Events and groups (40 seconds)

1. Open the **Events** tab. Show **For You** against **All**, and tap a filter chip such as **Virtual**. Clear it.
2. Open **Midtown Morning Coffee Meetup**. Show the map pin, the RSVP buttons, and the **guest list**. Tap a guest you haven't met, such as **Sofia Marin**, to open their full profile with an **Ask for a chat** button. Go back twice.
3. Tap **Host** to show the form: title, type, date, location, capacity, public or community-only. Close it.
4. Tap the **Groups** tab. Join a group, then open one to see its members and events.

## 4:40 Feedback, thank-yous and profile (40 seconds)

1. **Demo → Trigger a post-chat check-in.** Answer *Did you meet?*, give a thumbs up and pick a tag. Stress that these answers are **private and never shown on a profile**.
2. Scroll to **Send a thank-you** and type one line. This is the one part the other person sees. "Research shows a thank-you is what brings a mentor back."
3. Under **What next with Kwame?**, choose **Check in monthly** and submit. Open the Network tab: Kwame has moved out of "Not in your web yet" and into the web. Open his chat to show the thank-you card and his reply.
4. **Demo → Receive a thank-you note.** The Profile tab opens on **Thank-you notes**, which only Riley can see.
5. **Demo → Show account-warning notice.** Show the gentle notice on Profile and tap it to open the community guidelines.
6. On Profile, point out edit profile, chat capacity, groups, events and **Pause my profile**.

## Closing: any school (10 seconds)

1. **Demo → Switch school → Emory.** Flip through Find, Events and Network. Everything is now Emory.
2. **Demo → Switch user** to show the alum view.

"Kudzu works for any school. Schools are data, not code."

---

## If something goes wrong

- **Stuck or cluttered state:** Demo → **Reset all demo data**, then **Skip onboarding (demo)**.
- **Find shows nobody:** tap **Clear all**, or remove a filter.
- **Map is grey:** map tiles need an internet connection. The pins and vines still draw without them.
- **No confirmed chat for the check-in:** propose a coffee chat in any conversation first. It is accepted automatically.
