# Sources

Every source cited in this folder, with its grade, a link, and how far it was read.

**How to read the "Checked" column**

- **Full text** means the relevant passage of the paper itself was read.
- **Abstract** means the claim was confirmed against the paper's abstract or its indexed record.
- **Summary** means the claim comes from a university, publisher or press summary, or from a search summary of the paper. It was not confirmed against the paper.
- **Not re-checked** means the source was carried over from an earlier round and not opened again in this one. Where an earlier round did read it, the last table says how far.

Grades are explained in the [README](README.md#how-strong-is-the-evidence).

Two cautions apply to the whole list. Most sources were read as abstracts or summaries, not in full. Some citation details (volume numbers, page ranges, a few DOIs) are flagged in the "Checked" column as unconfirmed and should be verified before anything here is published.
