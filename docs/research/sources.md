# Sources

Every source cited in this folder, with its grade, a link, and how far it was checked.

**How to read the "Checked" column**

- **Abstract** means the claim was confirmed against the paper's abstract or its indexed record.
- **Full text** means the relevant passage of the paper itself was read.
- **Summary** means the claim comes from a university, publisher or press summary of the paper, not the paper.
- **Memory** means it was cited from prior knowledge and not confirmed in this round.

Grades are explained in the [README](README.md#how-strong-is-the-evidence). Add new sources at the bottom of the relevant section.
